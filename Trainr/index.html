Your week,
written for you

Tell Trainr your goal, your experience and when you can train. It writes a full week around you, then builds the next one from what you actually lifted.

  • Weight loss, muscle gain, strength, endurance or general fitness
  • As many days as you have, sessions as long as you have

The AI in Trainr

Bad day?
Tell the AI.

Open Adjust today on any session and say what's going on: tap a reason, or type it in your own words. The AI in Trainr reads your note right on your phone, shows you what it understood, and changes the workout only when you say so.

  • Less time? It keeps what matters and cuts the rest
  • No bench today? It swaps the move
  • Something hurts? It stops you pushing through and helps you decide
  • Or type it: on-device AI reads your note, and nothing leaves your phone
At home or at the gym

Built for
what you’ve got

Pick the equipment you actually own, from a full gym to nothing at all. The week is written with that, and only that.

  • Bodyweight, dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, plates, bands and machines
  • Kilograms or pounds, with separate units for the plates in your gym
  • Change your kit or your days any time and start a fresh plan
Every set, every video

Know the move.
Beat last week.

Every exercise comes with a video and the muscles it hits. Last week’s numbers sit beside today’s, so you always know what to beat.

  • Log weight, reps and time for each set as you go
  • A built-in timer for planks, intervals and timed work
  • Tick off sets and watch the session fill in
  • Two clear ways to end: log the whole session, or save what you’ve done
